# Welcome to Access Collins

Access Collins is a hospitality venue booking management platform built by The Access Group. It helps venues manage reservations, customer data, and day-to-day operations — all from a single system.

## Who are these docs for?

These docs are for **external integrators**: third-party developers, venue web developers, and partners who need to integrate with Collins. Whether you are building a server-to-server integration or embedding a booking form on a website, you will find everything you need here.

## What can you do with Collins?

- **Manage bookings** — create, update, retrieve, and delete bookings via the API.
- **Manage customers** — maintain customer records, marketing preferences, and contact details.
- **Query venues** — retrieve venue information, areas, and availability.
- **Embed a booking widget** — add a booking form directly to your website for customers to make reservations.
